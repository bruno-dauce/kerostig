Call for papers

Search for current calls for papers in your subject area

Use our search tool to find call for papers in your subject area, in 2 simple steps.

Step 1: Select the relevant check box/es and press ‘Load’.

Step 2: Explore the results across Taylor & Francis, Routledge, and Cogent journals.

If a particular journal doesn’t show, it doesn’t mean it isn’t accepting submissions. Rather the journal is promoting a special theme or topic for a limited time.

If you’re looking to publish open access, we’ve got plenty of information here on Author Services.

Loading time for results

The results may take a minute or more to load. We appreciate your patience while the information is being retrieved. Thank you for your understanding.
